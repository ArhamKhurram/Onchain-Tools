The fastest path to a useful console.

## 1. Sign in (hosted only)

On the hosted web console, click **[ SIGN IN ]** in the top-right. You can
continue with Discord or use email + password. Signing in syncs your rooms and
settings across devices. On the desktop app there's no sign-in — you're in
[local mode](./hosted-vs-local/) and everything is stored on your machine.

## 2. Connect Discord

This is the one step everything else depends on. Head to
**Settings → Tokens** (or the Feed page will prompt you) and paste your Discord
token. Full walkthrough, including how to find the token:
[Connect Discord](../../connecting/discord/).

Optionally [connect Telegram](../../connecting/telegram/) too.

## 3. Enable your servers

Servers are **off by default** so the channel picker stays manageable. Go to
**Settings → Guilds** and enable the ones you want to watch. Only enabled
servers show up when you build a room.

## 4. Build a room

A **room** is a set of channels streamed together. Open the Feed, hit **⌘K**
(Ctrl-K), choose **NEW ROOM**, name it, and pick the channels. See
[Rooms & channels](../../feed/rooms/).

## 5. Watch, then tune

- The [Feed](../../feed/feed/) now streams your channels live.
- Every contract mentioned flows into [Callers](../../callers/contracts/), and
  [Radar](../../callers/radar/) ranks tokens by how many callers are on them.
- Track a few [FOMO traders](../../fomo/overview/) and turn on the
  [alerts](../../alerts/notifications/) you want — toasts, sounds, or phone push.

## The five modules at a glance

| Module | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Feed** | Live Discord/Telegram chat, aggregated into rooms |
| **Callers** | Contracts detected in your feeds + the Radar ranking |
| **FOMO** | Live trades from fomo.family traders you follow |
| **Portfolio** | PnL/holdings/activity for your own wallets |
| **Directory** | A watchlist of on-chain wallets to monitor |
