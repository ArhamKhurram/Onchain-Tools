## The modules

OCT is organized into modules you reach from the top nav:

- **Feed** — live Discord/Telegram chat, aggregated into rooms.
- **Callers** — every contract address mentioned in your feeds, plus a Radar that ranks tokens by crowd.
- **FOMO** — live trades from the fomo.family traders you follow.
- **[Sniper](./sniper/sniper/)** — a pre-declared buy you fire yourself, behind caps and a kill switch. The one module that spends money; read its safety notes first.
- **Portfolio** — PnL, holdings, and activity for your own wallets.
- **Directory** — a private watchlist of on-chain wallets to monitor.

Plus a **Workspace** to build your own multi-column dashboard, and **Settings**
for everything else.
